# RoboLab Free Core Policy

The following core capabilities are intended to remain free for users:

- Building / Robot Builder AI
- Code Generator AI
- Circuit Generator AI

The free core should support serious robotics project generation and iteration rather than being deliberately crippled to a demo. Premium features, if introduced later, should focus on infrastructure or convenience such as higher usage limits, faster inference, advanced simulation/hosting, collaboration, storage, or additional compute—not removal of the fundamental ability to build.

## Reliability policy

"Competition-ready" means RoboLab can target a competition-grade engineering process. It must not claim that a physical robot is competition-ready until applicable rules, safety checks, hardware testing, and real-world validation are completed.

## Model honesty

The 24-role training suite contains training infrastructure and starter datasets. It does not claim that 24 frontier neural networks are already trained. Actual model weights require a pretrained base model, training data, compute, and evaluation.
